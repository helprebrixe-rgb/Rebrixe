How the Audit Scoring Works

Not all SEO issues are equal. This tool weights each check by real-world ranking impact. Critical items like missing title tags, absent canonical links, or broken robots directives carry 3× the penalty of structural improvements. Warning-level issues carry 1.5× weight. Your score reflects the actual severity of your site's technical health — not just a raw pass/fail count. Fix the red items first. Always.
