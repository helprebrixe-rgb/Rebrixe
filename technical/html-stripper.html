Why use an HTML Stripper?

Copying text from a browser shows rendered output — but when you grab raw source or CMS exports, you're buried in tags, scripts, and noise. This tool rips all of that out and hands you clean, pasteable text in one click.

Scraping & data cleaning

Got a web scrape full of HTML tags? Strip them in bulk and get just the content. No regex headaches, no manual find-replace loops.

CMS & email template work

Email editors and CMS platforms love injecting spans and divs. Paste the exported HTML, strip it, and copy clean text back in.

Content migration

Moving content between platforms? Strip the old HTML structure before pasting into your new editor. Avoids phantom formatting nightmares.

SEO audits

Check what text Google actually indexes by stripping the page source. No scripts, no nav — just the readable content signals on the page.
