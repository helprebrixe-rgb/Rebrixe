# Keyword Count Density

Mastering Keyword Distribution

Search engines use keyword density as a signal for topical relevance. However, modern SEO is about natural language. Our tool helps you identify over-optimization before you publish — showing single keywords, bigrams, and trigrams so you understand how search engines actually read your content.

What's the ideal keyword density?

Most SEO experts recommend staying between 1% and 2%. Anything above 3.5% risks being flagged as keyword stuffing. This tool uses those exact thresholds to color-code your results.

What are stop words?

Common words like "the", "and", "is", "a" that carry no SEO value. Our analyzer filters over 150 stop words so the results only show meaningful keywords that actually affect your rankings.

Why analyze bigrams & trigrams?

Multi-word phrases (like "digital marketing strategy") are how people actually search. Analyzing 2 and 3-word combinations shows you how your content looks to modern semantic search engines.

What is the Pin Keyword feature?

Enter your target keyword and instantly see its exact count, density, and whether it's in the safe zone — without hunting through the results table manually.

Is my content safe?

Yes. Everything runs 100% in your browser. Your content — which may be unpublished, confidential, or client work — never touches a server. No uploads, no logging.

Can I export results?

Yes. Download a CSV of the keyword table to share with a client, import into a spreadsheet, or keep as a record for your content audit workflow.
