Known Limitations — Read Before You Plan
  • No public holiday awareness. This tool only skips weekends. It does not know about national holidays (Christmas, Diwali, US Thanksgiving, etc.). You must manually shift dates around holidays.
  • Review and Retrospective fall on the same calendar day (the last working day of each sprint). Your team will need to schedule them as separate time slots on that day.
  • Next Sprint Planning is placed on the first day of the following sprint. Some teams prefer to hold planning on the last day of the previous sprint — adjust manually if needed.
  • Date calculations use your browser's local time zone. There is no timezone selector. If your team spans multiple time zones, treat output dates as local to whoever generates the calendar.
  • "Current sprint" detection uses your device's clock. If your device date/time is wrong, the highlighted sprint will be wrong too.
  • No calendar export (Google Calendar / Outlook / iCal). Use the Markdown, CSV, or JSON export and import manually.
  • Sprint start dates that fall on a weekend are accepted as-is. The tool does not auto-shift your chosen start date — pick a weekday start.
  • On 1-week sprints, the Mid-Sprint Check-in and Retrospective may fall on the same day — there are only 5 working days and 3 events to place. Both are shown so you can schedule them as separate time slots.
