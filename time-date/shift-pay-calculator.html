Shift Pay Calculator

Enter your shift, rates & breaks — get your exact pay breakdown.

⚠ What this calculator does — and doesn't do
  • Handles shifts that cross midnight (e.g. 22:00 → 06:00).
  • Splits paid hours between day and night rates based on your night window.
  • Handles one unpaid break per shift, deducted evenly from day and night hours.
  • Applies different rates for weekdays, Saturdays, Sundays, and public holidays.
  • Supports overtime as a flat hourly rate after a paid-hours threshold — within this one shift only.
  • Weekly mode totals seven independent shifts. Each day is calculated in isolation.
  • Tax is not calculated. Removed because a flat-rate percentage gives false precision and varies greatly by country, income bracket, and allowances. Use your payslip or a dedicated tax tool.
  • Split shifts (two separate time blocks in one day) are not supported — one continuous start → end per day only.
  • Weekly overtime accumulation across multiple days is not calculated. OT applies per shift, not across the week.
  • Night rate is not forced to be above the day rate. You set whatever rate applies. We warn you if they conflict, but we won't override your input.
  • Currency conversion is not performed. All inputs are in the same currency you select.
