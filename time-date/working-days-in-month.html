⚠ Tool Limitations — Please Read
  • Working days = Monday–Friday only. The tool has no concept of custom work weeks (e.g. Sunday–Thursday).
  • Holiday data is approximate. Public holidays are computed from fixed rules and algorithms. Edge cases (substitute holidays when a holiday falls on a weekend, regional proclamations, regional variants) are not modelled.
  • India holidays are national gazette holidays only (3 fixed dates + New Year). State-level and floating religious holidays (Holi, Diwali, Eid, Onam, Pongal, etc.) vary by state, religion, and year — they cannot be reliably hardcoded. Add them via Custom Days Off.
  • UK holidays are England & Wales only. Scotland and Northern Ireland have different bank holiday schedules.
  • Australia holidays are national public holidays only. State-specific holidays (e.g. Melbourne Cup Day, Queensland Show Day) are not included.
  • Billing hours are configurable below but default to 8 h/day. The tool calculates hours as working days × your chosen hours per day — it does not account for partial days or overtime.
  • Time zones are not considered. All dates use your browser's local date. If your team spans time zones, results may differ by one day near month boundaries.
