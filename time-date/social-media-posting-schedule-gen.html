What this tool does — and doesn't do
  • One platform per schedule. This tool generates a schedule for a single selected platform. If you post across multiple platforms, run it separately for each one.
  • One post per day, maximum. Scheduling multiple posts within the same day (e.g. 2× daily) is not supported. Each date in your schedule represents exactly one post.
  • No posting times. This tool gives you dates only — it does not pick or recommend what time of day to post. Consult your platform's analytics for optimal posting windows.
  • Posts are spaced evenly across your active days. When your requested frequency doesn't divide evenly into your selected days, the tool picks the closest evenly-spaced dates. You may get 1 fewer or 1 more post than the exact arithmetic implies.
  • No content suggestions. This is a date-planning tool. It does not suggest what to post, generate captions, or track engagement.
  • No account integration. This tool does not connect to any social platform. It generates a calendar you then follow manually or import into your own scheduling app.
  • Daily (7×) posting with fewer than 7 active days selected will silently cap your total posts to the number of available days in range. The tool will note the actual count in the stats.
  • No automatic reminders. Export to CSV and import into Google Calendar or a scheduling tool like Buffer / Later if you need push notifications.
