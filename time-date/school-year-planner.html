⚠ What this tool does & doesn't do
  • Weekends are fixed as Sat–Sun. The tool always treats Saturday and Sunday as non-school days. Mon–Fri school weeks are assumed. There is no option for different weekend structures (e.g. Fri–Sat).
  • No public holiday auto-fill. The tool does not know your country's bank/public holidays. You must add every holiday manually as an event.
  • Overlapping events: if two events overlap on the same day, the calendar shows only the higher-priority one (Exam > Holiday > Break > Term). All event names still appear in the tooltip.
  • Day-count stats count calendar weekdays within each event range — not net school days after subtracting other events. A term day inside an exam period counts as both a term day and an exam day.
  • No save / account system. Data is not saved between sessions. Refresh the page and your plan is gone.
  • Print output uses your browser's built-in print dialog. Layout quality depends on your browser and printer settings.
