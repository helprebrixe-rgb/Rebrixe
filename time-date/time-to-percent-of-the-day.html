Time → % of Day

3:00 PM = 62.5% through the day. For data viz, scheduling math & progress tracking.

⚠ Tool Limitations — read before using
  • Business hours are hardcoded as 9:00 AM – 5:00 PM. You cannot change them. The "% of Work Day" stat always reflects this fixed 8-hour window.
  • No timezone support. All calculations use your device's local clock. The tool has no knowledge of timezones, DST shifts, or UTC offsets.
  • No overnight (cross-midnight) spans. If your day start is later than your day end (e.g. 10 PM → 6 AM), the input is rejected. The tool only handles same-day windows.
  • Seconds are not a direct input. Time inputs are hours and minutes only. The Live mode tracks seconds internally, but you cannot manually enter a time with seconds.
  • Percentages are rounded to 2 decimal places in the display. The raw fraction (6 decimal places) is available in the Stats panel if you need higher precision.
  • Clipboard copy requires HTTPS or localhost. The copy buttons will not work on plain HTTP pages — this is a browser security restriction, not a tool bug.
  • No date awareness. The tool treats every calculation as occurring within a single abstract day. It does not know the calendar date or account for leap seconds.
