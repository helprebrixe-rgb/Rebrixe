Team Availability Heatmap

Add teammates + time zones → see who's free when, instantly.

⚠ What this tool does & doesn't do — read before using
  • Hourly resolution only. Half-hour and 45-minute timezone offsets (IST UTC+5:30, Nepal UTC+5:45, Adelaide UTC+9:30, Iran UTC+3:30…) are rounded to the nearest whole hour. Cells may be off by 30–45 min for people in those zones.
  • Today's DST rules apply. Offsets are computed from right now. If a teammate's DST changes next week, this grid won't reflect that until you reload.
  • No cross-midnight shifts. Work windows must start and end on the same calendar day (e.g. 9 AM–6 PM). Overnight shifts (e.g. 10 PM–6 AM) are not supported.
  • Work hours apply per person at add-time. You set hours before adding each person. You cannot edit them after — remove and re-add to change.
  • No data is saved. Refreshing the page clears everything. This tool has no accounts or persistence.
  • Max 10 teammates — beyond that the heatmap becomes unreadable anyway.
