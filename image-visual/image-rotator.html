Why this tool?

Photo came out sideways?

Phones sometimes save EXIF rotation data that apps ignore. One click fixes it permanently — no more "why is it sideways everywhere?"

Expand vs Crop

Expand grows the canvas to fit the full rotated image — perfect for odd angles. Crop keeps the original dimensions and trims the corners.

Any angle, not just 90°

Drag the slider or type any value from −360° to +360°. Useful for straightening a slightly tilted scan or horizon.

100% private

Everything runs in your browser via the Canvas API. Your image never touches a server. Works offline too.
