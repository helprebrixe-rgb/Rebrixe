How & when to use this

Whiteboard & document photos

Shot a whiteboard or paper at an angle? Drag the 4 corners to the document's edges and the tool warps it to a straight rectangle — like a mobile scanner, right in your browser.

Architectural photography

Buildings shot from below look like they're leaning back. Place the 4 corners on the building's corners and correct the keystoning without any desktop software.

Receipts & business cards

Drag corners to the receipt edges, hit Correct — you get a clean overhead view, great for archiving or reading text that was hard to see at an angle.

How the warp works

Uses a projective transform (homography) computed from your 4-point selection. The output maps those corners to a flat rectangle using bilinear interpolation on the Canvas API. Zero server, zero upload.
