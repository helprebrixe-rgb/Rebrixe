About this converter

Why PNG → JPG saves so much space

PNG uses lossless compression, great for quality but heavy on disk. JPG's compression algorithm cuts file sizes by 50–80% with barely visible quality loss — perfect for photos and web images.

Background colour matters for transparency

JPG has no transparency layer. Any transparent area must be filled with a solid colour before converting. Use white for documents, black for dark-themed content, or pick a custom colour to match your site.

Quality presets explained

Web (60%) is ideal for fast-loading pages. Balanced (92%) is indistinguishable from lossless at half the size. Max (100%) is JPG's best — still smaller than PNG for photos.

100% private — zero upload

Everything runs in your browser using the Canvas API. Your image never leaves your device, no server involved. Works offline once the page loads.
