How it works

Why does my photo look yellow?

Indoor tungsten and LED bulbs emit warm light that cameras struggle to neutralise. Dragging the Blue channel up (and Green slightly) kills the yellow cast instantly.

Blue shadows in outdoor shots?

Shade and overcast skies bounce blue skylight into shadows. Reduce Blue, lift Red slightly. The "Blue Shadows" preset handles this in one click.

Use the histogram

The live RGB histogram shows where each channel peaks. If red towers over blue and green, your photo is warm/yellow — balance by lifting blue.
