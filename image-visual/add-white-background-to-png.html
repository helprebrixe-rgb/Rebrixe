Why your PNG looks black — and how this fixes it

The "black PNG" problem

Apps that don't support transparency (WhatsApp previews, JPG converters, some email clients) render transparent areas as black. Adding a white background before saving fixes this permanently.

Preparing PNG for JPG

JPG format has no alpha channel — it can't store transparency. Converting a PNG directly to JPG without flattening first will always produce black blotches. Always flatten first.

Pick any background color

Choose white for documents and printing, #000 for dark-mode assets, or any custom hex color. Great for matching brand backgrounds before export.

100% private, zero upload

Everything runs in your browser using the Canvas API. Your image never touches a server. Works offline once the page loads.
