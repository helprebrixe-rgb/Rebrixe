How it works

K-means colour clustering

Samples pixels from your logo and groups similar colours together using k-means clustering — the same technique used in design tools to find dominant brand colours.

Tints & shades formula

Each extracted colour is mixed toward white (tints) and toward black (shades) in equal steps, giving you a complete 50–900 style scale for every brand hue.

Skip neutrals option

Whites, blacks, and near-greys dominate most logos. Toggle Skip near-white/black on to surface the actual brand accent colours instead of background fills.

100% private, zero upload

Everything runs in your browser via the Canvas API. Your logo never touches a server. Works offline once the page loads.
