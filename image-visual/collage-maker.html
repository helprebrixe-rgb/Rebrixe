How to use the Collage Maker

30+ ready layouts

Pick from grids, feature panels, magazine spreads, and story formats. Tabs filter by photo count so you always see the right options.

Custom grids

Need 7 or 9 photos? Switch to Custom tab, set your columns and rows, and the tool builds your grid automatically.

Drag to reorder

Uploaded photos show as thumbnails. Drag them into the order you want — the collage slots fill in that sequence.

High-res download

The collage renders at 2× resolution internally (up to 3200px wide) for crisp prints and social media posts.
