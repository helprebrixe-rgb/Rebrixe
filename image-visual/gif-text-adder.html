How it works

Text on every frame

The tool decodes every frame of your GIF, draws your text on each one using the Canvas API, then re-encodes the whole thing — so text stays locked in sync with the animation.

Classic meme font

Impact with a black outline is the OG meme style. Pick Impact from the font list, set outline to 3–5px, and you get the authentic look instantly.

Drag to position

Click and drag the yellow dot on the preview to place your text exactly where you want it — or use the 9-point position grid for quick snapping.

100% private, zero upload

Everything runs in your browser. Your GIF never touches any server. Works offline once the page has loaded. No watermarks, no account needed.
