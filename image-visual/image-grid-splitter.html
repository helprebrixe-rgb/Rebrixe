Why this splitter?

Instagram carousel grid

Split a wide panoramic or square image into a 3×3 or 4×4 grid. Each piece becomes one carousel slide — when viewed on your profile grid, they form one seamless image. Massive engagement hack.

Multiple grids supported

2×2 (4 tiles), 3×3 (9 tiles), 4×4 (16 tiles), plus asymmetric 2×3 and 3×2 options. Works with any image — portrait, landscape, or square.

One click ZIP download

Grab all pieces as a .zip file in one click — already named in the correct order. No juggling individual downloads.

100% private

Uses the browser Canvas API — your image never touches a server. Works offline once loaded. No account, no watermark, no nonsense.
