How it works

Decimal precision

Rotate to 0.1° increments — perfect for fixing the subtle 1–2° tilt that ruins an otherwise great shot. Use the nudge arrows for hair-fine adjustments.

Rule of thirds grid

Toggle the grid overlay while rotating so you can align horizons, buildings, and subjects to the guidelines — no guessing, no re-editing later.

Auto-crop removes white corners

Auto-Crop automatically trims the rotated image to the largest rectangle with no empty corners. Your final image is clean with no black or transparent edges.

100% private

Everything runs on the Canvas API inside your browser. Your photo never touches a server. Works offline once the page is loaded.
