How it works

Reposition, not just crop

The frame stays fixed at your chosen ratio — you drag and zoom the image underneath it, so your subject lands exactly where you want in the composition.

Power points & snapping

The four grid intersections are where the eye naturally lands. Drag near one and the image gently snaps into place — toggle off for freeform control.

Non-destructive editing

Rotate, flip and zoom are all reversible before you commit. Nothing is written to pixels until you hit Crop & Preview.

100% private

Everything runs in your browser via the Canvas API. No upload, no server, no storage. Works offline after page load.
