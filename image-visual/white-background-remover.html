How it works

Luminance flood-fill removal

Samples the corner pixels to detect background colour, then removes all connected pixels within the tolerance range — similar to Photoshop's Magic Wand tool, but tuned for white studio shots.

Tolerance controls what's "white"

At 30 only near-pure white is removed. Raise to 60–80 for blown-out, overexposed backgrounds. Lower to 10–15 for fine detail-heavy products.

Feather for smooth edges

Edge feathering blurs the alpha channel on the cutout boundary. Removes jagged pixel edges and makes the product look naturally cut out — essential for eCommerce listings.

Spill removal kills white fringe

White backgrounds "bleed" onto product edges. Spill desaturates and darkens semi-transparent edge pixels so there's no white halo on dark site backgrounds.

Shadow Preserve

Keeps soft grey tones beneath your product. Natural drop shadows make products look grounded on a page — raise this if your photo has a subtle product shadow you want to keep.

Always outputs transparent PNG

PNG is the only lossless format that supports transparency. The file never leaves your browser — everything runs client-side using the Canvas API.
