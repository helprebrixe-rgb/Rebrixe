Why HEIC is a pain — and how this helps

What is HEIC?

HEIC (High Efficiency Image Container) is Apple's default photo format since iOS 11. It's ~50% smaller than JPG at equal quality — great for storage, terrible for sharing with non-Apple users.

Why can't Windows open HEIC?

Windows and most web platforms don't support HEIC natively. Converting to JPG fixes compatibility with every device, browser, email client, and editing app instantly.
