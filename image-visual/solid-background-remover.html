How it works

Click-based flood fill

When you click, the tool samples that exact pixel colour, then erases all connected pixels within your tolerance range — just like Photoshop's Magic Wand tool, but instant in your browser.

Tolerance controls reach

Low tolerance (10–20) removes only very similar colours. High tolerance (60+) erases a wider shade range — useful for off-white or slightly uneven backgrounds.

Pick multiple colours

Click in multiple spots before hitting Remove. Great when your background has slight gradients or shadows — pick the dark corner AND the bright centre, then remove all at once.

Edge Feather for cleaner cuts

Feather softens the boundary between your subject and the removed area. Set to 1–2 for crisp logos, 3–5 for slightly organic edges on product shots.

Always downloads as PNG

PNG preserves the alpha channel (transparency). Your removed background stays transparent — paste it anywhere: Canva, PowerPoint, Figma, or your website.

100% private, zero upload

All processing uses the Canvas API entirely in your browser. Your image never leaves your device. Works offline after the page first loads.
