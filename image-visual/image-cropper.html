How it works

Drag-handle cropping

Powered by Cropper.js — drag corners, edges, or the crop box itself. Pinch-zoom works on mobile too.

Aspect ratio lock

Pick 1:1 for profile pictures, 16:9 for YouTube thumbnails, 9:16 for Reels & TikTok — or go fully free.

Live crop coordinates

X, Y, Width, Height update in real-time as you drag. Essential for developers or anyone needing precise pixel control.

100% private

Everything runs in your browser via Canvas API. No upload, no server, no storage. Works offline after page load.
