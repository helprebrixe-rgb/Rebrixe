Why you need this

Text readability on photos

A dark overlay at 40–60% opacity turns any busy background photo into a canvas where white text is perfectly legible — the standard trick for hero banners and Instagram stories.

Brand colour tinting

Apply your brand colour at low opacity (15–30%) to unify a mix of stock photos. Suddenly every image looks like it belongs to the same campaign.

Gradient overlays for cinematic feel

Bottom Fade is the secret weapon for video thumbnails — darken the lower half so a title card sits perfectly at the bottom without obscuring the main subject.

Blend modes for mood

Multiply deepens shadows for drama. Screen lifts highlights for a dreamy look. Color tints without affecting brightness — great for duotone effects.

Vignette / Feather

The Feather slider fades the overlay toward the centre of the image, drawing the viewer's eye inward — classic portrait and product photo technique.

100% private, zero upload

Everything runs via the Canvas API in your browser. Your photo never leaves your device. Works offline once the page loads.
