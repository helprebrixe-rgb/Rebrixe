How to use

Region Blur

Draw a rectangle over any part of the image — a face, password, address, or background. Stack multiple regions. Each one is undoable individually.

Pixelate mode

Choose Pixelate for a mosaic-style blur — great for hiding faces in thumbnails without looking like a mistake.

Radial blur

Places a blur centered on where you click. The area around that point stays sharp. Great for a "tilt-shift" or focus effect on product shots.

100% private

All processing uses the browser's Canvas API. No server, no upload. Your screenshots with sensitive data stay on your device.
