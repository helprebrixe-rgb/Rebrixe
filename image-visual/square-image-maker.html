Why square matters

Instagram & profile photos

Instagram crops profile photos to a circle from a square frame. Non-square images get awkwardly cropped. Use 1080×1080 for feed posts and 320×320 for profile pictures.

eCommerce product shots

Most marketplaces — Amazon, Etsy, Shopify — require or strongly prefer square product images. 800×800 or 1080×1080 are safe choices for most platforms.

Crop vs Pad — which to use?

Crop Center trims the sides to fill the frame — great for portraits and tight shots. Pad with Colour adds borders to preserve the full image — best for products and logos.

Blur Background trick

The blurred background method gives a polished, modern look — no harsh edges, no white box. Your image fills the frame and the blurred version of itself fills the padding, keeping the whole square on-brand.
