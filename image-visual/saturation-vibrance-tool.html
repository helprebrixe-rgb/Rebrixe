Saturation vs Vibrance — what's the difference?

Saturation boosts everything

Cranking Saturation up intensifies all colours equally — great for landscapes but can blow out skin tones and make photos look unnatural fast.

Vibrance is smarter

Vibrance only boosts colours that are already muted, and protects warm skin tones. The go-to slider for portrait and influencer content that needs to pop but still look real.

Split preview saves time

Drag the divider in the Split view to compare before and after side-by-side without toggling. Great for subtle adjustments where you're not sure if you've gone too far.

100% private, zero upload

All processing uses the browser's Canvas API. Your photo never touches a server. Works offline once the page loads.
