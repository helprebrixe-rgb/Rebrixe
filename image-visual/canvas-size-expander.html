Why designers love this

Breathing room, not distortion

Unlike resize tools, this adds empty space around your image. Your original pixels stay 100% untouched — only the canvas gets bigger.

9-point image placement

Place your image anywhere — top-left, center, bottom-right and everything in between. Perfect for asymmetric layouts or leaving space for text overlays.

Transparent PNG support

Select Transparent background and export as PNG to get a canvas-expanded image with true alpha — great for Figma, Photoshop, or web overlays.

Quick pad presets

Hit +40, +80 etc. to add equal padding to all sides instantly. Then fine-tune individual sides if needed. Saves 30 seconds every time.
