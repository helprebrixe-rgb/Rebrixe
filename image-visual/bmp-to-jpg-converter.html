Why convert BMP to JPG?

BMP files are gigantic

A 1920×1080 BMP is around 6 MB uncompressed. The same image as JPG at 88% quality is under 300 KB — a 95% reduction. Great for email, web, storage.

Batch convert all at once

Drop as many BMPs as you want — screenshots, scans, exports from legacy software. Convert and download them all in a single ZIP. No size limits.

WebP for even smaller files

Choose WebP to get images 25–35% smaller than JPG at the same visual quality. Perfect for websites and modern apps.

Completely private

Everything runs inside your browser using the Canvas API. Your files never touch a server. Works offline once the page loads.
