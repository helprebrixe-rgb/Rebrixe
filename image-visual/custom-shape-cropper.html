How it works

Transparent PNG output

All shapes export as PNG with a fully transparent background — paste directly onto any design, post, or presentation without white boxes.

Smart Crop vs Fit Inside

Smart Crop fills the shape completely (may trim edges). Fit Inside shows the whole image — ideal for logos or product shots you don't want cropped.

Padding for breathing room

Add padding (px) to shrink the image inside the shape — great for circular profile pics where faces get cut at the edges.

100% private, zero upload

Uses Canvas API entirely in your browser. Your image never touches a server, works offline once the page is loaded.
