Why use Bulk EXIF Viewer?

What is EXIF data?

EXIF (Exchangeable Image File Format) is metadata your camera embeds in every photo — camera model, lens, shutter speed, ISO, GPS location, and the exact date/time it was shot.

Sort & filter shoots instantly

Sort 500 photos by camera, ISO, focal length or date in one click. Find every shot taken above ISO 3200 without opening Lightroom. Perfect for culling shoots.

Privacy check before sharing

See which photos contain GPS coordinates before posting them online. The GPS column lights up so you can strip location data before uploading anywhere.

Export to CSV for reporting

Photographers managing client shoots or stock libraries can export all metadata to CSV and import it directly into Excel, Airtable, or Notion for archiving.
