Why this matters

Your photo contains your home address

Every photo taken on a modern smartphone embeds your GPS coordinates — latitude and longitude accurate to a few metres. Anyone receiving the original file can open it in any EXIF viewer and see exactly where you were.

What is EXIF data?

EXIF (Exchangeable Image File Format) is metadata baked into photos by cameras and phones. It can include GPS location, timestamp, camera make/model, lens info, serial numbers, and even the device owner's name.

When to strip before sharing

Before posting on social media, sending via email, listing items for sale, sharing in public forums, or submitting photos professionally. Most platforms strip EXIF — but not all, and not always instantly.
