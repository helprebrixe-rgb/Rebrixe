How it works

Unsharp Mask — the pro method

Used in Photoshop and professional workflows. Creates a slightly blurred copy, then adds the difference back — this amplifies edges making the image look crisper.

Strength controls intensity

Higher strength = more dramatic sharpening. For portraits keep it under 120 to avoid noise. For text or documents, 180+ gives crisp results.

Threshold avoids noise

The Threshold slider tells the filter to skip pixels that are similar to their neighbors. Raise it to protect skin tones and skies from grain.

100% private, zero upload

Everything runs in your browser using the Canvas API. Your image never touches a server. Works offline once the page loads.
