Why use this?

Pixel-perfect ratios

Lock to 16:9, 9:16, 1:1, 4:3, 3:2 or any custom ratio. The crop box is mathematically constrained — no guesswork.

Drag to frame the shot

Drag the crop box to nail composition. Use corner handles to resize while keeping ratio locked. Rule-of-thirds grid helps you frame like a pro.

Rotate & Flip built in

Shot sideways on your phone? Rotate 90° before cropping. Flip horizontally or vertically without opening Photoshop.

100% private, zero upload

Everything runs inside your browser with the Canvas API. Your image never touches a server. Works offline once loaded.
