How it works

Great for logos & mockups

Transparent PNGs look invisible on white slides. Add a textured pattern background so your logo pops in presentations, pitch decks, and social posts.

6 pattern types

Dots, grid, horizontal lines, diagonal lines, crosshatch, and noise — each rendered procedurally in the browser. No external assets needed.

Canvas padding adds breathing room

Increase the Padding slider to give your logo space on the canvas — useful when the original PNG has very tight crop.

100% private, zero upload

Everything runs via the Canvas API. Your image never touches a server. Works offline once the page loads.
