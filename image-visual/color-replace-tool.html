How it works

Click to pick any colour

Hover over your image — a magnifier helps you land on the exact pixel. Click and the source colour is sampled instantly. No manual hex guessing needed.

Tolerance matches similar shades

A Tolerance of 30 replaces colours within 30 units of your picked colour. Raise it for gradients and shadows; lower it for precision on flat graphics.

Stack multiple replacements

Replace one colour, then replace another — each step is tracked in the history log. Undo any individual step, or reset to original in one click.

Edge Blend for clean results

The Edge Blend slider applies a soft transition at colour boundaries — eliminating the harsh jagged edges you get with simple flood-fill tools.

Great for logos & product shots

Swap a brand colour across a logo export, change a t-shirt colour in a product mockup, or recolour a background object — without Photoshop.

100% private, zero upload

All processing uses the browser's Canvas API. Your image never leaves your device. Works offline once the page has loaded.
