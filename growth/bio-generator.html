Bio Optimization Guide

The 'I Help' Formula

The highest-converting bios follow: "I help [Target Audience] achieve [Result] using [Method]". Clarity always beats cleverness when you want someone to follow or buy.

Emojis as Bullets

Emojis aren't just decoration — they're visual bullet points that break up text, save characters, and add personality. Place them at the start of each line for maximum scannability.

Always End with a CTA

The last line of your bio should direct visitors: "Grab the free guide 👇", "DM for collabs", or "New video every Tuesday". Bios without a clear next step waste the click.

Platform Character Limits

Instagram: 150 chars. Twitter/X: 160 chars. LinkedIn: 220 chars. TikTok: 80 chars. YouTube: 1000 chars. Threads: 150 chars. Each platform punishes overflow — stay tight.
