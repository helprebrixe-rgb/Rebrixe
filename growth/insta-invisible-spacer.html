HOW IT WORKS:

Instagram silently deletes empty lines when you paste a caption. This tool injects an invisible Unicode character (U+2800 Braille Pattern Blank) into every blank line. The character is completely invisible to readers but registers as "content" — so Instagram, TikTok and Threads preserve your spacing exactly as intended.

Why use an Invisible Spacer?

The Problem

Instagram's algorithm automatically collapses empty lines when you paste a caption. This destroys carefully crafted paragraph breaks, ruins aesthetic layouts, and makes long captions incredibly hard to read. Creators spend extra time manually adding dots, dashes, or emojis just to fake spacing — which looks unprofessional and clutters the caption.

The Solution

We use the U+2800 Braille Pattern Blank Unicode character. It is completely invisible to human readers but treated as active text content by social platforms. Inserting it into each blank line tricks Instagram, TikTok, and Threads into preserving your exact formatting — no visible hacks, no workarounds, no compromises on your caption's appearance.

No More Dots

Stop using ugly periods (.), dashes (-), or random emojis just to separate your paragraphs. Those visible spacers look amateurish, especially for brands and professional creators. Our invisible spacer keeps your caption layout 100% clean. Your audience sees only your words, your formatting, and your intent — nothing else. It's the professional way to write captions.
