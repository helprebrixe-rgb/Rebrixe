Why Title Case Matters for YouTube SEO

Higher Click-Through Rate

Videos with properly capitalized titles consistently earn a higher CTR because they look authoritative and intentional — not like amateur uploads. A 1% CTR bump can be the difference between a video YouTube promotes or ignores.

AP Stylebook Rules Built In

Our engine follows professional editorial standards: connectors like "a", "and", "in", "for", and "the" stay lowercase unless they open or close the title. Words after a colon or em-dash are auto-capitalized because they start a new thought.

The YouTube Algorithm Effect

Capitalization is not a direct ranking signal, but human behavior is. Professional titles earn more clicks, longer watch time, and higher shares — all signals the algorithm uses to decide whether to recommend your video to new viewers.

Five Modes for Every Use Case

Beyond Title Case, choose Sentence Case for conversational topics, ALL CAPS for urgent hooks, lowercase for relaxed aesthetics, or Alternating Case for meme-style thumbnails. One tool handles every format your content needs.

100-Character Limit Warning

YouTube allows up to 100 characters in a video title. Our live counter tracks your length in real time and turns amber at 80 characters and red at 100, so you never accidentally cut off your title in search results or notifications.
