The Retention Formula

Negative Bias

Humans are 2× more likely to click on a warning than a tip. Framing content as "Stop doing X" outperforms "How to do X" in nearly every A/B test. Loss aversion is wired into us — use it.

The 1.5s Rule

If your hook text takes longer than 1.5 seconds to read, the viewer has already scrolled. Keep it under 8 words on screen. Every word you cut increases your watch time.

Curiosity Gap

The brain hates incomplete information. Hooks that promise a reveal ("Here's what no one tells you…") exploit this hardwiring. The viewer must watch to close the loop.

Direct Callout

Addressing a hyper-specific person ("If you're a freelancer in your 20s…") feels personal. Niche callouts get more saves and shares because people tag exactly who it describes.
