Why decouple social tags from live platforms?

Testing metadata structures locally cuts out the tedious deployment-and-refresh cycle. Seeing your cards rendered visual-style inside a local container stack ensures alignment errors, layout truncation points, and generic placeholders are caught instantly — before they ever reach a live audience. A broken card on LinkedIn or a missing image on WhatsApp costs real clicks and credibility.

What is the business impact of clean OG metadata?

When high-intent users distribute your tools or page nodes organically across text channels or direct posts, optimized meta assets provide an immediate point of visual authority. Beautiful cards reliably lift organic referral loops. Studies consistently show that link posts with compelling OG images receive 2–3× more clicks than bare URL pastes. The image is the hook — the title closes the deal.

Character limits matter more than you think

Every platform applies its own truncation rules. Google Search clips titles around 60 characters and descriptions at roughly 160. X truncates descriptions at ~200 characters. LinkedIn shows around 150. Writing to the tightest constraint — 60 for titles, 155 for descriptions — ensures your message lands clean everywhere, without awkward mid-word cuts that destroy comprehension and reduce click-through rates.

The 1.91:1 image ratio — why it's non-negotiable

Facebook, X, and LinkedIn all center their unfurled card image renders around the 1.91:1 aspect ratio. Deviating causes cropping that removes key visual elements, often cutting out faces, product shots, or branding from the frame. Always export your OG image at exactly 1200×630 pixels, keep important content in the safe center zone, and host the file on a CDN with public access — bots cannot scrape images behind authentication walls.
