Why Silo Mapping Matters

Topical Authority

Google doesn't rank single pages anymore; it ranks sites that cover a topic comprehensively. Silos prove you're an expert across the entire subject cluster.

Link Juice Distribution

Internal links flow strategically upward. Supporting posts push authority to your money pages via optimized anchor text, compounding your ranking signal.

Search Intent Coverage

Each tier targets a different intent — commercial, informational, and transactional — ensuring you capture buyers at every stage of the funnel.

Crawl Efficiency

A clean silo structure makes it trivial for Googlebot to discover, index, and understand the hierarchy of your site — fewer orphaned pages, better crawl budget.
