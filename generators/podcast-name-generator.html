How to Choose a Binge-Worthy Podcast Name

01. Niche Focus Be specific. 'The Tech Podcast' is generic; 'Silicon Valley Secrets' is a brand.
02. Length Check Ideally, keep it under 4 words. Long names get cut off in mobile podcast apps.
03. Availability Always check if the social handles and domain are available before committing.
