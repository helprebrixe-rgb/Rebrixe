Stream Optimization FAQ

Visual Hierarchy

Twitch browse feeds are dark. Using brackets [ ] or pipes | helps break up the text, making it 20% easier for viewers to read quickly.

The 'Stakes' Meta

High-stakes titles like "I lose = I gift subs" are the highest performing in 2026. They give the viewer a reason to stay.

Progress Tracking

Instead of just 'Playing Valorant,' try 'Road to Radiant - Game 1/10.' Viewers love joining a journey in progress.
