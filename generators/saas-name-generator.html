How to Build a Modern SaaS Identity

01. Sticky Suffixes Suffixes like -ly, -ify, and -io are standard because they turn a noun into an action.
02. Technical Phonetics Names with K, T, and P sounds are often easier to remember and sound more "precise."
03. Scalable Logic Avoid naming your app "SmallInvoice" if you plan to handle enterprise data later. Keep it broad.
