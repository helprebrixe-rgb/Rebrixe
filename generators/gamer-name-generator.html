Dominate the Lobby with a Pro Name

01. Character Limit Aim for 5-12 characters. Longer names often get truncated in kill-feeds and leaderboards.
02. Pronunciation If a shoutcaster or teammate can't say your name in 2 seconds, it's too complex.
03. Special Symbols Use symbols like "v" or "x" sparingly. Overuse can make a tag look dated or cluttered.
