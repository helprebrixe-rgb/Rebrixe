How to Choose a Winning Clan Name

01. Tag Potential Ensure your name can be shortened into a cool 3-letter tag. Example: "Shadow Vanguard" becomes [SVG].
02. Avoid Clichés Stay away from overused words like "Pro" or "Elite" unless you have a unique twist.
03. Group Synergy Pick a name that fits the whole group. If your team plays tactical shooters, go for grounded military names.
